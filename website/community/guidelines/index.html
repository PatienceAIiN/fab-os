Community standards

Keep it useful.

These guidelines help keep the Fab OS community welcoming, practical, and safe.

What belongs here

  • Share useful questions, ideas, feedback, project notes, and images related to Fab OS.
  • Be kind and discuss ideas without attacking people.
  • Share only material you created or have permission to publish.
  • Protect your privacy. Never post passwords, API keys, private contact details, or sensitive personal information.
  • Do not post malware, unlawful content, harassment, spam, impersonation, or instructions intended to cause harm.

Moderation and ownership

You can edit or delete only posts created in your browser. Other visitors cannot alter your post; moderation may remove content for safety, abuse, spam, copyright, or legal requests. Use the report control on a post or email info@patienceai.in for moderation concerns.
