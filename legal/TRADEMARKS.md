# Trademarks

## Ubuntu and Canonical

Fab OS is derived from Ubuntu. **Ubuntu is a registered trademark of
Canonical Ltd.** Fab OS is an independent project of Patience AI and is
**not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Canonical Ltd.**

In line with Canonical's Intellectual Property Rights Policy, Fab OS images:

- do not use the Ubuntu name as part of the product name;
- do not ship Canonical's logos, wallpapers, Plymouth themes, GRUB themes,
  Ubuntu fonts as branding, or the "Circle of Friends" mark;
- replace `/usr/lib/os-release`, `/etc/lsb-release`, `/etc/issue`,
  `/etc/legal`, the MOTD and the dpkg origin so the running system identifies
  as Fab OS;
- may state factually that Fab OS is "based on Ubuntu 26.04 LTS".

Package names, repository paths and the `UBUNTU_CODENAME` field are unchanged
because they are functional identifiers required for software compatibility,
not branding. Canonical's policy permits this.

## Fab OS and Patience AI

"Patience AI" and the Fab OS mark (the woven ring logo) identify software
published by Patience AI. You may:

- redistribute unmodified Fab OS images and refer to them by name;
- state that your software "runs on" or "is packaged for" Fab OS;
- use the logo to link to the project.

You may not use the name or logo for a modified image, a fork, or any product
in a way that suggests it is published or endorsed by Patience AI. Forks must
choose their own name and logo (this repository makes that a one-line change
in `brand/brand.conf`).

**Naming status:** "Fab OS" is not a registered trademark of Patience AI and
Patience AI claims no registration it does not hold. The search recorded in
`TRADEMARK-SEARCH.md` (2026-09-11) found no registered software mark for
"Fab OS" in the sources checked; the earlier conflict concerned the previous
name "Fabric OS". A formal clearance search and an Indian Class 9 application
are still recommended before a wide public launch.
